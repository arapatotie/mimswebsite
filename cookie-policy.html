Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how MIMS PH and its services use cookies and similar technologies on our website. Cookies help provide essential functionality, improve site performance, and deliver analytics that help us improve content and user experience.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They can be persistent (stored for a defined period) or session-based (deleted after the session ends).

Cookies we use

  • Essential: Required for navigation, security (e.g., CSRF tokens), and core features of the site.
  • Performance & Analytics: Aggregated usage data (for example, Google Analytics) to understand traffic and improve pages. These do not identify you personally.
  • Preferences: Remember language, font-size, or other display preferences to improve your experience.
  • Third-party content: Embedded content (videos, social widgets) may set cookies controlled by those third parties; we do not control their cookie policies.

How long we keep cookies

Cookie duration varies: session cookies are removed when you close your browser; persistent cookies remain for a predefined period (typically 30 days to 2 years) depending on their purpose.

Managing and opting out

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. To opt out of analytics cookies, you can use browser add-ons provided by analytics vendors (for example, Google Analytics Opt-out). Disabling certain cookies may limit functionality.
