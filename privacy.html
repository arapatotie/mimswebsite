Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to MIMS PH (“we”, “us”, or “our”). MIMS PH provides a Medical Information Management System and related public-facing content. This Privacy Policy describes what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights in relation to that data.

We process personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and applicable data protection laws. This policy applies to information we collect through our website and related services at / and under the MIMS PH domain.

2. Information we collect

  • Account & Contact Data: name, email address, professional affiliation, and any profile information you provide when you register for site features or newsletters.
  • Health-related inputs: clinical values or symptoms you enter into calculators or search forms. These inputs are treated as sensitive and are not stored unless you explicitly save them to a private account.
  • Technical Data: device type, browser, IP address, timestamps, and cookies used to operate the site and analyze performance.
  • Communications: correspondence you send via contact forms or email (e.g., support requests).

3. How we use your information

We use data to:

  • Deliver and improve our services, features, and content personalization.
  • Respond to your requests, comments, and support inquiries.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations and cooperate with regulators or law enforcement where required.

4. Legal basis & sensitive data

Where applicable, we rely on your consent, legitimate interests (e.g., site security, analytics), contractual necessity, or legal obligations to process personal data. Health-related inputs are treated as sensitive personal data and will only be processed with explicit consent or when necessary for the specific functionality you requested.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who host the site, process payments, or provide analytics (under data processing agreements).
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law (for example, to comply with subpoenas or reporting obligations).
  • Affiliated partners only with your consent (e.g., to deliver professional services or verify credentials).

6. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to provide essential site functionality, remember preferences, and measure performance. Third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) may collect aggregated usage data. See our Cookie Policy for details and opt-out instructions.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described, to comply with legal obligations, or to resolve disputes. Health inputs entered into calculators are not retained by default unless you choose to save them in an authenticated account.

8. Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards such as HTTPS/TLS encryption, access controls, secure hosting, and periodic security reviews to protect personal data. No system is completely secure; if you suspect a breach, contact us immediately.

9. International transfers

Some service providers may operate outside the Philippines. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we require appropriate safeguards (e.g., standard contractual clauses) to protect your data.

10. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal data. To exercise your rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at the address below. We will respond within the timeframes required by law.

11. Children

Our site is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without parental consent. If you believe we have collected a child’s information, contact us to request removal.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted with a revised effective date. Continued use of the service after changes indicates acceptance.
